# 🔬 Research Agentic AI

An AI-powered Research Agent that acts as an intelligent research companion for
students, researchers, and professionals.

The application uses the **Qwen qwen/qwen3.8-27b model through the Groq API**
to provide interactive research assistance, structured research insights, and
research analysis.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Researchers and students often deal with scattered academic papers, technical
articles, research notes, and other information across multiple sources.

Finding relevant information, analyzing research content, identifying research
gaps, tracking emerging trends, and discovering future research directions can
be time-consuming.

The Research Agent aims to simplify this process using Agentic AI.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

The Research Agent provides an intelligent research companion that helps users:

- Explore and refine research topics
- Analyze research information
- Generate literature insights
- Identify research gaps
- Generate research questions
- Analyze emerging trends
- Generate academic search strategies
- Suggest future research directions
- Analyze research documents/PDFs
- Interact with an AI research assistant

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📚 Literature Landscape
Provides structured insights into major themes, concepts, and research areas.

### 🔍 Research Gap Identification
Helps identify possible unexplored areas, limitations, and open research
questions.

### 📈 Emerging Trends
Highlights current and upcoming trends related to a research topic.

### 🧠 Knowledge Graph
Represents important concepts and relationships between research topics.

### 🚀 Future Research Directions
Suggests potential areas and opportunities for further research.

### 🔎 Search Strategies
Generates useful academic keywords and search queries.

### 💬 Agent Chat
Provides interactive research assistance using natural-language queries.

### 📄 Document Analysis
Allows research documents/PDF content to be analyzed and converted into
structured insights.

### 🛡️ Safety & Guardrails
Includes protection against prompt injection, system-prompt disclosure,
API-key exposure, and unsupported or fabricated research claims.
